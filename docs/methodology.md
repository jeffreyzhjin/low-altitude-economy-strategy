# Methodology

## Decision question

Which low-altitude economy scenarios should a Chinese internet or technology platform enter first?

The hypothetical decision owner already has demand aggregation, users, merchants, payments, maps and location data, cloud and AI capabilities, dispatch systems, and operations teams. It does not start with aircraft manufacturing or airline-operation capabilities.

## Scenario boundary

Six scenarios cover consumer logistics, B2B services, public-sector operations, critical logistics, passenger mobility, and enabling digital services:

1. Urban instant delivery
2. Low-altitude digital infrastructure and services
3. Industrial inspection
4. Urban governance and emergency response
5. Medical and emergency logistics
6. Tourism and passenger mobility

The boundary follows the official low-altitude economic classification and the application areas named in national policy. Scenarios are grouped to keep the comparison useful for one platform-level decision.

## Scoring criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Market demand | 20% | Is the customer problem frequent, costly, or urgent? |
| Platform capability fit | 25% | Can existing users, merchants, maps, cloud, AI, dispatch, and operations create an advantage? |
| Commercialization clarity | 20% | Is the payer, value metric, and revenue path identifiable? |
| Regulatory feasibility | 20% | Can a limited pilot operate with realistic permissions, insurance, and safeguards? |
| Data and network effects | 15% | Does usage improve routing, risk, supply, or matching in a defensible way? |

Each criterion uses a 1–5 ordinal judgment:

- 1: materially unfavorable
- 2: weak
- 3: mixed or conditional
- 4: favorable
- 5: strongly favorable

The weighted score is:

`score = demand × 20% + fit × 25% + commercialization × 20% + regulation × 20% + data × 15%`

Scores are not probabilities, market sizes, or investment returns. A 0.1-point difference should not be treated as a precise economic gap.

## Evidence handling

The source ledger separates verified facts from their use in the decision. Official policy and regulator statistics receive the highest evidence strength. Company operating figures are useful for commercial validation but remain self-reported. Historic cases show mechanisms, not current market scale.

## Sensitivity analysis

The code compares the base case with three alternative strategy profiles:

- **Growth platform:** more weight on demand and platform fit
- **Compliance first:** more weight on regulatory feasibility
- **Asset light:** more weight on platform fit and commercialization

A robust recommendation should remain near the top under more than one plausible profile. If a scenario moves sharply, the final decision should focus on the assumptions causing the change.
